## Executive Summary

1. **Next.js security release published 2026-11-03.** Patched in 16.4.5 and 15.5.29. Seven advisories: one High, six Medium. Our pin `16.2.6` is behind. A grep of `frontend/src` and the Next config found **no use** of `use cache`, `unstable_cache`, Cache Components, Edge runtime routes, `NextResponse.cookies` or `dangerouslyAllowSVG`. Most advisories therefore appear not to apply to us. Exposure is likely limited to the image-optimizer SVG bypass, which needs a permitted image source and a native backend that decodes SVG. This is an inference from grep, not a full audit. Upgrading is still recommended and needs human approval.
2. **Correction to earlier claim.** A search snippet mentioned an unauthenticated image-optimizer RCE (Hacktron, 2026-10-22). The primary post does not list it, and says the SVG bypass does not itself demonstrate XSS or RCE. Treat the RCE claim as unconfirmed.
3. **Upstream search results are thin and partly stale.** XyFlow and FastAPI releases found are from July 2026. Nothing was found for React, TypeScript, Tailwind, SQLModel, Alembic or RQ. Absence of results is not evidence of no change.
4. **Open issues unchanged.** Agent-ready issues are still blocked on human PostHog setup.
5. **UX research:** only marketing-grade and review-site material. Themes are consistent with the current product direction (see Implementation References).
