### #510 — Pydantic → Zod codegen seam
**Confirmed dormant, plus one new data point.** `@dmitryrechkin/json-schema-to-zod` (zero commits/releases since 2025-03-10) and `pydantic2zod` (last push 2025-05-28, last release Nov 2024) remain stale, unchanged. Two new candidates checked this cycle: `zod-to-json-schema` (StefanTerdell) is explicitly being **sunset** by its own maintainer now that Zod v4 ships native `z.toJSONSchema()`/`z.fromJSONSchema()`; `openapi-zod-client` (astahmer, last push 2025-02-10) is also stale and is OpenAPI→Zod rather than Pydantic→Zod, an even weaker fit. No new tool has emerged. **Refined recommendation:** hand-written Pydantic `@model_validator`/`@field_validator` → Zod `.refine()` pairs remain the plan, but Zod v4's native JSON-Schema support plus Pydantic's own `model_json_schema()` is a mildly-relevant new candidate seam worth a line in the eventual spec, in place of any third-party codegen dependency.

### #511 — Atomic `submit()` / commit-then-enqueue seam
**Correction to prior characterization, not just re-confirmation.** `hyzyla/outbox-streaming` is still dormant (zero commits since 2023-02-05, never tagged a release) — but its own project roadmap lists **RQ + SQLAlchemy support as Planned, not implemented**; today it only ships Kafka and Celery integrations. This makes it a weaker match than prior cycles assumed (prior reports treated it as a "candidate," now confirmed it never had the relevant capability at all). No other Python/RQ/SQLAlchemy-specific outbox library or article surfaced. **Recommendation, now firmer:** a hand-rolled outbox relay is required — this isn't a fallback from a stale-but-relevant tool, it's the only option, since no tool in this ecosystem has ever shipped RQ support.

### #334 — PostgreSQL 18 evaluation
PG19 still Beta 2, unchanged since 2026-07-16, no Beta 3. **New:** a write-heavy sysbench benchmark (smalldatum.blogspot.com, June 2026) shows PG17/18 regressions of 10–17% on several write-heavy patterns vs. PG15 (large-server config), tentatively attributed to MVCC GC/mutex contention — with insert-heavy throughput as the one improvement (+10% on PG19 beta1). See Findings for Active Issues above for the read most relevant to Blockbuilders' ingest-heavy workload.
