## 4. Security risks

Unverified unless noted. Confirm each against the official advisory and our pinned versions.

1. **React Server Components (frontend).**
   - CVE-2026-23864 (January 2026) is patched in 19.0.4, 19.1.5 and 19.2.4.
   - CVE-2026-23870 is a DoS (OOM or CPU), patched in 19.0.6, 19.1.7 and 19.2.6.
   - CVE-2026-23869 (April 2026) was reported only by a low-quality blog.
   - Background: CVE-2025-55182, a CVSS 10 RCE in RSC (fixed in 19.0.1, 19.1.2, 19.2.1), was added to CISA KEV on 2025-12-05.
   - Action: confirm the installed `react`, `react-dom` and `next` are at or above the patched versions.
2. **Redis server.**
   - A May 2026 advisory covered five CVEs, including CVE-2026-23479 and CVE-2026-25243.
   - A September 2026 Red Hat advisory covers CVE-2026-66373 (RCE via crafted RESTORE) and CVE-2026-81934 (use-after-free in TLS).
   - A Canadian Cyber Centre notice from 2026-08-28 lists fixed releases 8.2.9, 8.4.6, 8.6.6 and 8.8.2.
   - Action: check the Redis image tag in docker-compose (dev and prod). Redis holds the OAuth state, rate limits and the RQ queue, so the exposure matters. Ensure it is not reachable beyond the compose network.
3. **PostgreSQL.**
   - Reported CVEs include CVE-2026-6476 (SQL injection, fixed in 18.4/17.10) and CVE-2026-6479 and CVE-2026-6637 (CVSS 7.5 and 8.8).
   - Medium issues dated 2026-10-09 are fixed in 18.5/17.11/16.15 or later.
   - Action: confirm the minor version of the postgres image and move to the latest minor.
4. **Ecosystem tools.** PgBouncer 1.26.1 (2026-10-14) fixes an authentication bypass (CVE-2026-107350). Only relevant if PgBouncer is used; nothing in the files I read suggests it is.
5. **Gaps.** No advisory data was found for FastAPI, Starlette, SQLModel, Alembic, RQ or XyFlow. Run `pip-audit` and `npm audit` in CI. Auth, billing and migrations are human-gated, so any fix touching them goes to research or plan only.

## 5. Implementation references

UX patterns for visual strategy builders (product descriptions, not formal studies):

- **Vantixs:** node-based builder that pairs backtests with walk-forward and Monte Carlo checks, and surfaces risk and assumptions before deployment. Our Health Bar and cost metrics already cover similar ground. The transparency angle is a candidate follow-up.
- **Backtrex:** indicator blocks joined by conditions, reporting Sharpe, Sortino and drawdown. This matches our model.
- **MQL5 EA-Builder:** conditions written as near-plain sentences, with visual AND/OR groups. This is relevant to the Strategy Explanation Generator (§4.8) and to the plain-language validation messages.
- **AlgoAlpha (TradingView):** form-based entry/exit conditions combined with AND/OR, with no canvas. It is a useful reference for the mobile and wizard flows.
- **Known limit:** visual builders struggle with multi-timeframe, portfolio and custom-execution logic. This is consistent with our one-asset, one-timeframe, long-only constraints.
