This is a *structural canvas constraint*, contrasting with Blockbuilders' own Entry/Exit/Risk health-bar, which is a *completeness indicator* layered on an otherwise-unconstrained canvas rather than a hard zone boundary. Worth noting as a design-choice contrast, not a gap. Source: [backtrex.com/en/blog/no-code-trading-platform-beginners-2026](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/no-code-trading-platform-beginners-2026).
