### Issue #618 — NL-wedge onboarding A/B (`ready-for-agent`, **blocked**)
**Blocker:** HITL #620 — configure `onboarding_ab` PostHog experiment flag, metrics, and guardrails.
**Status: new reference material, no state change.** Chat2Workflow itself (arXiv 2604.19667) has no new revision since v2 (2026-05-26). But three new-to-corpus references surfaced this cycle:
- *Backtrader-Bench* (arXiv 2608.11232, submitted 2026-07-31, FinLLM Workshop @ IJCAI 2026): tool-augmented LLM trading-code agents hit 90.0% accuracy vs. 73.0% without tools; on harder decontaminated questions, half the tested models without tool access fell to ~25% (chance level). Directly supports keeping execution-grounded validation (the existing compile→validate→repair→decline pipeline, issues #625/#626) over trusting raw NL→strategy output.
- *Artic* (arXiv 2608.21341, submitted 2026-08-21): artifact-driven NL-workflow compiler using pre-execution "local obligation" faithfulness checks and scenario-based dry runs; reports +28pp task-resolve rate vs. raw NL workflows. Closest thing to a Chat2Workflow successor found to date, though not a direct revision of it.
- *VisCanvas* (arXiv 2607.21886, v2 2026-08-09): node-based LLM-authoring UX study (n=20) — AI output modeled as persistent, revisable/branchable graph nodes rather than linear chat replies, with more diverse exploration at comparable usability vs. chat-only baseline. Transferable design pattern for surfacing NL-generated strategy edits directly on the existing canvas.
- Product-side: Make.com's **"Maia"** launched 2026-08-12 — a conversational AI co-worker embedded in the visual Scenario Builder that narrates edits step-by-step and asks clarifying questions, still strictly AI-drafts/human-edits. No canvas-UX change found from n8n (still the Oct-2025 AI Workflow Builder beta), Zapier (August news was pricing/tiering only), Coinrule/CoinruleGPT (no update found), or TradingView (no first-party NL-to-strategy feature; third-party Pine Script tools report ~85% NL→code success but are not a TradingView product change).
- No paper or product anywhere claims NL-to-trading-strategy reliability "solved" — if anything, Backtrader-Bench reinforces the opposite. Opened 2026-06-11, now **87 days idle**.
