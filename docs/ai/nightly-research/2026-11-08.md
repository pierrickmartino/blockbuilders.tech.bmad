## Executive Summary

1. **Next.js is still the top risk.** The pin `16.2.6` is the May 2026 security release. Newer security releases followed in July, September and early November 2026. The latest, reported by Pantheon on 2026-11-03, covers seven issues (one high, six medium) and lists 16.4.5 as the patched version. The version numbers come from one vendor page and need checking against the official advisories. Upgrading needs human approval.
2. **Version numbers conflict across reports.** Earlier reports gave 16.3.8, 16.4.1 and now 16.4.5 for Next.js, and 0.141.1 versus 0.162.0 for FastAPI. The search index is inconsistent. No target version should be chosen until someone checks the primary pages.
3. **No advisories were found for the other dependencies.** The broad CVE search returned only IBM, Ubuntu, PHP and Spring items. That is not evidence that FastAPI, Starlette, Redis, RQ or PostgreSQL are clean.
4. **Open issues are unchanged since 2026-11-03.** The three `ready-for-agent` issues are still blocked on human PostHog setup.
5. **UX research is vendor-grade only.** It confirms the current design direction and adds nothing new to adopt.
