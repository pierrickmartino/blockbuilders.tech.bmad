## Executive Summary

1. **The Next.js 16.4.5 release is now confirmed from the primary source.** The official post (published 2026-11-03) lists seven issues: one High, six Medium. The repo pin `16.2.6` is behind it. Upgrading needs human approval.
2. **Real exposure looks low from a code check.** A grep of `frontend/src` and `next.config.*` found no `use cache`, `unstable_cache`, Cache Components, Edge runtime routes or `next/image` use. That makes five of the seven issues unlikely to apply. The two that could apply to any `next start` deployment are the compressed-response framing issue (only when a fetched `Response` is returned unchanged behind an HTTP/1.1 proxy) and general hygiene. The app uses `output: "standalone"`, so it is self-hosted. This is a quick grep, not an audit.
3. **Earlier 2026 Next.js and React advisories (May, October) were not re-verified tonight.** An October report mentions critical RCE in RSC packages and Image Optimization. Whether `16.2.6` already includes those fixes needs checking against the official advisories.
4. **No advisories were found for FastAPI, Redis, RQ, SQLModel, Alembic or PostgreSQL.** The searches returned nothing relevant. That is not evidence they are clean.
5. **Open issues are unchanged.** The three `ready-for-agent` issues are still blocked on human PostHog setup.
