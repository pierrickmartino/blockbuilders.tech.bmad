## Security Risks

### P0 — Redis `7.4.9` image below CVE-2026-81934 fix (new)
Sources: [Redis advisory](https://redis.io/blog/security-advisory-cve-2026-81934.md), [Canadian Cyber Centre AV26-859](https://cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/redis-security-advisory-av26-859).

- Exposure is unassessed: check whether Redis TLS is enabled and whether the port is published outside the compose network.
