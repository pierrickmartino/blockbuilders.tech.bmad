## Implementation References

- **React Flow:** 12.12.0 resets provider store properties on unmount and suppresses an attribution warning when the pane isn't rendered. 12.11.x fixed node resizing beyond extents and tightened node/edge typeguards. All are patch or minor changes within our `^12.10.0` range. See [reactflow.dev what's new](https://reactflow.dev/whats-new/2026-07-06).
- **Beginner onboarding on a node canvas:** Raven generates a full strategy and converts it to a node canvas for fine-tuning. This is the same shape as our wizard-to-canvas flow and the NL-wedge test in #618. See [Raven write-up](https://dev.to/emilanielsen/building-a-no-code-trading-bot-builder-for-prediction-markets-raven-26fp).
- **Block categories and boolean logic:** Composer and Backtrex use one block per operation, entry/exit/filter categories, AND/OR combination, and a separate results view with equity curve and trades. See [Backtrex guide](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/visual-trading-strategy-builder-no-code) and [Composer review](https://dev.to/pickuma/composer-review-automated-trading-strategies-without-writing-code-lj3). Our §4.2 and §5.4 already cover this.
- **Metrics:** Guides list expectancy and profit factor next to Sharpe, drawdown and win rate. We do not compute either. This is a possible later product proposal, not a recommendation for now.
